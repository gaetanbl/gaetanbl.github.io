The GMA Experience

Even though my heart lies in robotics and AI, I chose to pursue a Mechanical and Control Engineering degree (GMA) at INSA Rennes. I truly believe that having a rock-solid engineering foundation helps me stand out from purely software profiles. The various internships I've completed capture the core spirit of GMA wonderfully.

At XSun, I tackled finite element analysis (FEA) and materials science. At ADDLAB (Decathlon), my focus shifted to advanced 3D modeling and additive manufacturing. During my time at Metallicadour (later acquired by Safran) and through my academic coursework, I got hands-on with the operation and control theory of industrial robotic arms. And of course, my latest R&D adventure at Scalian awaits you further down!

Beyond that, my curriculum gave me a deep appreciation for advanced control theory and physics—covering everything from Noise, Vibration, and Harshness (NVH) and Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) to advanced PID control concepts like soft sensors.

To cap it all off, my final year is all about international mobility and a graduation internship. After picking up a brand-new language from scratch in just a few months, I headed off to study in Brazil, diving headfirst into machine learning, advanced mathematics, and coding!

✈️ INS'Aérien Club

In January 2024, I founded a brand-new student association: INS'Aérien. My vision was to build an open, collaborative space where we could build custom FPV drones and learn together from scratch. Beyond mastering high-speed quadcopter design, mentoring members on flight dynamics, and filming major campus events (like Rock'N Solex with over 20,000 attendees!), it was an incredible crash course in entrepreneurship: handling communication, securing funding, and pitching the project. Having served as president for two years, I couldn't be prouder of the team—we've successfully built 3 amazing drones!
